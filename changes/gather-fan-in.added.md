`gather` fan-in under `agentl serve`: `gather.add` marks a part of a group when its run succeeds, and a `gather:` trigger (`group` pattern, `expect` count or items, `ttl`, `deadline` with `fire: partial | fail`) starts one run per group once the parts arrived, exactly once even with concurrent additions; missing parts go to `gather.partial` / `gather.failed` notifications, and `agentl gather ls` / `forget` show and reset the state (#217).
